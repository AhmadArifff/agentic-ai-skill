# agentic-brand style

## Shape
- Outlines: ink (#111318), 6 px at 1080p, round joins and round caps.
- Corners: 24 px radius on cards, 999 px pill on badges and code tags.
- Emblem geometry: concentric 4-segment OODA ring around a solid ink core disc.

## Colour
- Fills are flat and crisp; gradients are reserved for soft ambient background depth only.
- Primary cobalt (#2563EB) marks Builder nodes; verified emerald (#10B981) marks Independent Reviewer gates.
- Never use pure #FFFFFF as a full-frame background; use warm paper (#F4F1EA) or deep ink (#111318).

## Light and depth
- Flat editorial offset shadow (12 px X, 14 px Y, ink at 18%) on light-mode badges.
- Subtle radial lift glow behind hero cards on dark scenes.

## Don't
- No blurred drop shadows on vector marks.
- No invented metrics; only display real verified counts from the workspace.
- No static dead frames during middle shots; maintain subtle forward camera push.
